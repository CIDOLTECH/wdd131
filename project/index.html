Why CIDOL exists

Abuja's terrain rewards riders who plan ahead.

Between the flat lakeside paths of Jabi and the granite switchbacks above Bwari, one city holds almost every gradient an eBiker will meet. CIDOL exists to document that range honestly — real distances, real elevation gain, and straight advice on which eBike class actually suits each route.

Graded, not guessed

Every listed trail carries a measured elevation profile and difficulty rating, so new members know what they are signing up for before ride day.

eBike-specific advice

Motor type and battery range matter more on Bwari's climbs than on Jabi's flats. Our trail guide pairs each route with the eBike class it actually needs.

A standing weekly schedule

Three rides a week, same meeting points, same directors. Consistency is what turns a route list into a club.
