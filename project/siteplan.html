1. Site name

CIDOL Cycle Club

This name represents a club or group focused on eBiking in the Abuja area of Nigeria's Federal Capital Territory. "CIDOL" is used as the club's founding acronym/identity, and "Cycle Club" states its function plainly for search engines and new visitors alike. The name is short enough to fit a jersey, a nav bar, and a URL without truncation.

Optional domain availability (not purchased for this course project): cidolcycle-ebike.org

2. Site purpose

The site is a hub for regional eBiking around Abuja. It provides graded trail and route information, plain-language eBike buying guidance matched to those trails, and a membership request form so new riders can join scheduled group rides.

3. Scenarios

Two representative questions from the target audience — Abuja-area eBike owners and prospective owners — that drove the site's content decisions:

  • "Which eBike should I buy if I mainly want to ride the hills around Bwari, not just flat lakeside paths?"
  • "How do I find out when the next group ride is, and how do I actually join the club?"

4. Color schema

The palette is drawn from Abuja's own terrain: deep hill-forest green, laterite red-earth roads, and a brass/gold sun accent on a warm parchment background. This keeps the site visually tied to its subject rather than reaching for a generic tech palette.

7. Testing plan (W06 Project)
